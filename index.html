FAANG AI Chatbot Project — Telus International Abstract Semantic Language (ASL) is a formal framework for representing the meaning of an utterance independently of surface wording — the kind of structured representation that teaches a model what someone means, not just what they said. My linguistics background made this a natural fit: annotating intent, argument structure, and semantic roles at scale. What the experience confirmed is that data quality is a linguistic problem as much as an engineering one. Consistency in labelling, edge-case disambiguation, and knowing when two utterances are semantically equivalent — these decisions shape what the model learns.
